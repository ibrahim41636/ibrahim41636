## 2. Personal data we collect

### Information you provide through our forms

When you submit a service request or contact form, we collect:

- Your name and company name
- Email address and phone number
- Location or city
- The service you are interested in and the project details you describe

Please include in your message and attachments only the information we need to respond to your enquiry. Avoid sending sensitive personal data, or personal data about other people, unless it is necessary and you are entitled to share it.

When a form is submitted, we also record:

## 4. How form submissions are handled

1. When you submit a form, it is processed by a serverless function running on our hosting infrastructure.
2. The function checks the submission against our spam protections (Cloudflare Turnstile and a hidden "honeypot" field) and applies rate limiting based on your IP address.
3. The submission, including any attachments, is sent by email to Selorin's sales team through a transactional email provider.
4. A minimal lead record, without attachments, is stored so that we can follow up on your enquiry and, where applicable, transfer it to our customer relationship management (CRM) system.
5. Attachments are forwarded by email only. They are not stored on the website.
